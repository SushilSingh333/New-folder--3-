    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is real estate lead generation in Gurugram?

    It is the process of finding buyers and investors for a Gurugram project through targeted Google, Meta and LinkedIn campaigns, then checking each enquiry before it reaches the sales team. AAJneeti has run real estate lead generation in Gurugram for developers and channel partners since 2014.

    How do you make sure real estate leads in Gurgaon are genuine?

    Every enquiry is checked against our blocklist of 1 lakh+ known fake contacts. Your team tags each lead in the free CRM, our QC team cross-checks the tags, and fake, invalid and broker leads are replaced. Your contract guarantees 40 to 80 percent qualified leads, and any shortfall is refunded.

    How much does real estate lead generation in Gurgaon cost?

    Smaller budgets pay per lead, and larger budgets work on a monthly retainer with the ad budget paid separately. The cost per lead depends on the project type, micro-market and price point, so we share a quote after a free project review.

    How quickly can we start getting leads?

    Campaigns usually go live 3 to 5 days after the project review and approvals, and leads start arriving as soon as the ads run.

    Do you run pre-launch and EOI campaigns for new launches in Gurugram?

    Yes. We run pre-launch, EOI and new launch campaigns for developers and channel partners, building interest before launch and qualifying enquiries by budget, location and intent, so your team spends its time on serious buyers.

    What types of real estate leads do you generate in Gurugram?

    Residential, luxury, commercial and rental leads: apartments, villas, builder floors, offices, shops and investment properties, for new launch, under-construction, ready-to-move and resale projects.

    Do you work with channel partners or only developers?

    Both. Developers use us for launches and steady sales, and channel partners use us to generate their own buyer leads for the projects they sell in Gurugram and across Delhi NCR.

    Which new projects are launching in Gurugram in 2026?

    Major 2026 launches include Godrej Samaris (Sector 53), Oberoi Realty's Three Sixty North (Sector 58), Signature Global's Tonino Lamborghini Residences (Sector 71), SOBHA Crescent and Anant Raj's The Estate One (Sector 63A), Max Estates' The Terraces (Sector 36A), WAL Serenia 92 (Sector 92) and BPTP's The Boulevard at Green Oaks (Sector 70A). Check the RERA registration before you book.

    Which areas of Gurugram have the most new launches?

    In 2026, most new launches are on Golf Course Extension Road (especially Sector 63A), Golf Course Road, the Southern Peripheral Road, the Dwarka Expressway and New Gurugram.

    How do I choose the best digital marketing agency in Gurgaon for real estate?

    Ask four questions. Is a qualified-lead percentage written into the contract? Are fake and broker leads replaced? Can you see every lead in a CRM yourself? Is the team measured on cost per qualified lead rather than ad spend? At AAJneeti, the answer to all four is yes.
